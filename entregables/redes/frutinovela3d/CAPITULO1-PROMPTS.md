Son 10 clips. Los **diálogos** van en **Google Flow** (Veo genera la voz con los labios sincronizados). Las escenas **sin diálogo** (acción, ambiente, reacciones) van en **Dreamina** y **Kling**. **Reglas para todos:**

- Adjunta la imagen indicada (las fichas de Gemini o la escena E# si ya la tienes) como **imagen inicial** o **referencia**.
- Si la herramienta pide duración, elige la más larga gratis (5 a 10 s).

## Google Flow (Veo) · los 4 clips con diálogo · 2 por día
En Flow usa «Ingredientes» o «Imagen a video» con las fichas de los personajes que salen. Copia el prompt tal cual; el diálogo va en español entre comillas.

**F1** · Fichas: Aguacate y Doña Arepa
```
Vertical 9:16, 3D animated film style, cozy Colombian kitchen at night, warm light. Aguacate (avocado-headed man in beige sweater vest) bursts through the door breathless, holding up his phone, and shouts in Spanish with a fast high-pitched gossip voice: "¡Arepa! ¡Vi a tu Chorizo con otra!". Doña Arepa (woman with a golden arepa head, pink curlers, floral apron) drops her wooden spoon and answers shocked, in a dramatic Colombian telenovela voice: "¿Con otra? ¿Con quién?". Quick push-in on her face. No music, no subtitles.
```

**F2** · Fichas: Comi, Doña Arepa y Don Chorizo
```
Vertical 9:16, 3D animated film style, living room at night, warm lamp light, telenovela drama. Comi (villain diva with a magenta delivery-box head, golden crown, magenta suit, pink fan) fans herself next to a guilty Don Chorizo (sausage-headed man with a mustache, white guayabera) and says with a mocking diva voice in Spanish: "Y solo me quedo con el veintisiete por ciento." Doña Arepa, hands on hips, asks furious: "¿El veintisiete por ciento de qué?". Comi leans in close to her face and smiles: "De ti, mi reina." Dramatic zoom on Comi. No music, no subtitles.
```

**F3** · Fichas: Orbi y Doña Arepa
```
Vertical 9:16, 3D animated film style, living room bathed in a soft magical blue glow. Orbi (waiter with a glossy blue planet head, glowing ring orbiting it, black vest, blue bow tie) kneels next to Doña Arepa, who is lying on the sofa, offers her a handkerchief and says in Spanish with a young, kind, confident Colombian male voice: "Doña Arepa, levántese. Usted no necesita que la lleven." The ring around his head spins slowly. Slow camera arc around them. No music, no subtitles.
```

**F4** · Fichas: Orbi, Doña Arepa y Comi
```
Vertical 9:16, 3D animated film style, living room, triumphant warm light. Orbi helps Doña Arepa stand up, looks at the camera and says in Spanish: "Con domicilios propios usted se lleva sola. Y sin comisión." and winks. Then Doña Arepa, proud with arms crossed, points at the door and says firmly: "Chorizo… vete con ella." In the background Comi stamps her foot and storms out. No music, no subtitles.
```
